What to bring.

Questions

Good to know.

Where do I park?

Free street parking right out front. No permit needed.

Can you pick up and drop off my pet?

Yes, for an extra fee. Let us know in your request and we’ll confirm the price for your address.

How many pets do you look after at once?

Only a few, so each pet gets proper attention. We never overbook, which is why dates can fill up quickly.

Does my pet need to be vaccinated?

Yes. Dogs and cats need up-to-date core vaccines. Please bring the record to the meet & greet.

Can you give medicine?

Yes — tablets, drops and most routine medicine. Write down the dose and times and we’ll follow them exactly.

Going away soon?

Send your dates and we’ll reply within 24 hours to confirm availability and set up a free meet & greet.
